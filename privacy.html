MoneyFly ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance manager for iOS. We built it on-device first, which means your financial life is designed to stay on your iPhone. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have.

The short version. Your accounts, transactions, budgets, goals and balances are stored on your device. We don't ask for your bank login, we don't sell your data, and we never use your finances for advertising. The only feature that uses the cloud is Split Wisely, and only when you choose to share expenses with other people.

1. Data we collect

Stored locally on your device only

  • Financial transactions, accounts, credit cards, budgets, goals, savings, gold holdings and Zakat data you enter or import
  • Content of bank SMS messages you choose to let MoneyFly read — parsed on-device to create transactions, never transmitted to us
  • App preferences and settings
  • Subscription status, cached locally to unlock Pro features

Collected only if you use Split Wisely (optional)

  • An email address and display name used to create your Split Wisely account
  • Group and expense data you add to shared groups, which is visible to the other members of those groups
  • Basic technical data (such as timestamps) needed to keep shared balances accurate and in sync

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, transmit or store your bank credentials, online-banking passwords or full card numbers
  • We do not sell or rent your data to third parties
  • We do not use your financial data for advertising or build advertising profiles
  • SMS content is processed entirely on-device and is never sent to our servers
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites

3. How we use data

We use the limited data described above only to provide the app's features:

  • To display your accounts, balances, insights and net worth on your device
  • To keep shared Split Wisely groups accurate and synchronized between members
  • To verify your subscription entitlement
  • To respond to you when you contact support

4. Third-party services

  • Apple StoreKit — handles subscription purchases and renewals. Apple processes payment; Apple's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Supabase — used exclusively to power the optional Split Wisely feature. Shared group data is stored on Supabase's EU infrastructure (Frankfurt). See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • On-device / AI parsing — voice and SMS transaction parsing runs on-device where possible. No financial data is sent to any AI service without your action, and never silently.

5. Subscriptions

When you purchase a MoneyFly Pro subscription, Apple processes the payment. We record your subscription status (active or expired), linked to an anonymous transaction identifier, solely to verify your entitlement to Pro features. We do not store payment card details. See our Terms of Use for the full subscription terms.

6. Data retention & deletion

All local data is removed when you delete the app from your device. If you created a Split Wisely account, you can delete it at any time directly in the app via Settings → Delete Account. This signs you out, deactivates your login and anonymizes your profile so your name no longer appears to other members.

Shared group records you contributed to are retained in an anonymized form (shown as "Deleted user") so that other members' settlement history remains accurate. You may also request deletion by emailing us — we will action verified requests within 30 days. See the account deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because most data never leaves your device, you already control it directly. For data held for Split Wisely, contact us using the details below and we will help you exercise these rights.

8. Children's privacy

MoneyFly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
