Delete your account

You can delete your MoneyFly account and data at any time — directly in the app.

Most of MoneyFly needs no account. Your own money tracking lives on your device — deleting the app removes it. You only have an account if you used Split Wisely to share expenses with other people. These steps delete that account.

Delete in the app (recommended)

Open MoneyFly

Launch the app on the iPhone where you're signed in to Split Wisely.

Go to Settings

Tap the More tab, then open Settings.

Tap "Delete Account"

Scroll to the account section and choose Delete Account.

Confirm

Confirm the deletion. Your login is deactivated immediately and your profile is anonymized.

What happens to your data

  • Removed / deactivated: your login is disabled and you're signed out on your devices.
  • Anonymized: your display name and email are removed from your profile, so your name no longer appears to other members — you show as "Deleted user".
  • On-device data: your personal transactions, accounts and balances live on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Why some shared records are kept. If you shared expenses in a group, those expense records are retained in an anonymized form so the other members' settlement history and balances stay accurate. No personal identifiers of yours remain attached to them.

Prefer to delete by request?

If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We verify and action valid requests within 30 days.

A note on subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. If you subscribe to MoneyFly Pro, cancel separately in Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → MoneyFly. See our subscription terms for details.
