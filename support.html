Getting started in 4 steps

Add your accounts

Add your bank, cash and card balances. No bank login is needed — you're always in control of what goes in.

Turn on SMS automation

Grant permission so MoneyFly can read bank alerts on your device and file them as transactions automatically.

Set budgets & goals

Add a couple of budgets and a savings goal. Your dashboard and daily briefing update as you go.

Your financial data is stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes local data, so keep a device backup if you want to preserve your history. Only Split Wisely data (shared groups) is stored in the cloud so members stay in sync.
